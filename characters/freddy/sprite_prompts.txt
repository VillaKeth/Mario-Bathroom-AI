A game-accurate 3D-rendered Freddy Fazbear animatronic from Five Nights at Freddy's, in the official promotional render style. A friendly-but-uncanny mechanical showman bear with a rounded golden-brown plush body over a visible metal endoskeleton frame. Broad rounded head with two short rounded ears, a stubby muzzle with a lighter tan snout, a shiny black nose, small rounded endoskeleton teeth, rosy red circular cheeks, thick brown eyebrows, and large expressive glowing pale-blue eyes. Wears a small black top hat and a black bowtie at the neck, with two black buttons down his chest. Chunky jointed animatronic arms and stubby three-fingered hands, holding a silver microphone in one hand. Subtly worn plush texture with faint seams and exposed metal joints at the shoulders, elbows and knees. Full body head to toe, standing, centered, generous margin so nothing is cropped, soft cinematic studio key lighting, plain flat white background. Pose: gesturing with both hand while explaining, animated and expressive.
